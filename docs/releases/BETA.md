## Download Ocean

Download **Ocean-…-arm64.dmg** below. You do not need Xcode, Terminal, npm or the “Source code” downloads.

**Requires an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS 26 or later.** This first community beta is free and distributed directly through GitHub.

### Install

1. Open the disk image and drag **Ocean** to **Applications**.
2. Open Ocean from Applications and choose **Use Ocean**. Setup is automatic.
3. Use the wave icon in the menu bar for **Ocean Settings**, or open Ocean again.

You can also open the app inside the download and choose **Install & Open** to have Ocean copy itself to Applications.

### macOS security notice

This beta is **not Apple-notarized** and has no Developer ID distribution certificate. macOS may block it as an unidentified developer. Only approve a download you trust from this repository; follow [Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/open-a-mac-app-from-an-unidentified-developer-mh40616/mac). Do not disable Gatekeeper or run commands that remove download protections. A warning-free public release needs Developer ID signing and notarization later.

### Included

- Live Metal ocean on the desktop, screen saver and lock screen.
- Guided first launch, straightforward settings and an Applications installer.
- Local daylight/weather, favorite held times, cinematic daytime and moonlight.
- Balanced, Save Energy and Sharper performance choices.
- Pause/resume, optional Open at Login, one-click repair and restoration.
- Bundled help/privacy information and support details that omit IP/coordinates.

To stop or uninstall, choose **Turn Off Ocean** in settings. It restores the previous wallpaper/screen saver, turns off Open at Login and quits. Then move the app to the Trash if you want to remove it.

### Beta limits

Native wallpaper integration depends on macOS compatibility interfaces and may need repair after OS changes. Physical multi-display/Space behavior and clean-machine installation need wider beta feedback. Older macOS and Intel Macs are not supported by this download. Updates are currently downloaded from GitHub, rather than installed silently.

Weather uses Open-Meteo’s non-commercial endpoint and approximate IP location from IPWhois. No GPS permission or account is required; provider terms apply. Weather/location lookup starts after Use Ocean. No analytics or automatic support uploads are included. See the bundled **Help & Privacy** page for details and credits.

The `.sha256` file and `.json` build manifest are for anyone who wants to verify the download; they are not additional installers.
